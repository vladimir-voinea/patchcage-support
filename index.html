Patchcage

Support

Patchcage: Network Simulator is an offline iPhone and iPad game where you fix the network yourself. You patch the cords, bring the ports up, cut the VLANs, write the routes, and read the trace to find out why nothing moved.

Nothing to sign in to

The app has no account, no server and no network access. Your progress lives on your device.

Progress and reinstalling

Which levels you have solved, and which one you had open, are stored on the device. Deleting the app deletes them. There is no copy in the cloud, because there is no cloud.

A level that will not solve

Every level's answer is solved for and proved before the level ships, so a level that cannot be finished does not ship. If a number you can defend is refused anyway, send the level name and the number and it will be checked.

What the numbers mean

Every number the app shows carries a badge saying where it came from:

  • SIM measured by the simulator itself;
  • CALC computed by a model the app states;
  • SPEC quoted from the protocol;
  • MODEL read out of your own configuration;
  • SAVE saved on your device;
  • SAMPLE an example, not a measurement.

No number is shown without one. If you find one, that is a defect and it is worth telling us about.

The bench is a model

The networks are simulated, and they are a teaching model rather than a picture of any particular equipment. Patchcage is not advice for configuring a real network, it models no vendor's products, and nothing in the app ever reaches a network of any kind.

Accessibility

The bench can be driven entirely by VoiceOver, the synthesised sounds can be turned off, and Reduce Motion is honoured: the blinking stops rather than slowing down. If something is unreachable for you, say so and it will be fixed.
