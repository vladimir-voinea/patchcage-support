Patchcage

Privacy

Short version: nothing leaves this phone. Not once, not at all.

What the app collects

Nothing. Patchcage has no account, no login and no network request of any kind. There is no analytics, no advertising, no in-app purchase, no third-party SDK, no crash reporter and no diagnostics.

The app never opens a connection. It does not check for updates, ask whether it is online, or phone home on launch.

What stays on your phone

  • which levels you have solved, and which one you had open;
  • your preferences: sound, haptics, frame announcements, the view you prefer and the speed you run at.

That is stored in the app's own storage on the device, and nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes it. Nothing is backed up to a server, because there is no server.

The simulator runs on your device

The whole simulation runs on your phone. No frame, no configuration, no answer, no failure and no measurement is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere to send it. The numbers the app shows are computed on the device and badged with where they came from.

The two links in the app

The privacy policy and the support page are links. They open in your browser, and only when you tap them. The app itself opens no address and makes no request on its own.

Purchases

The app is bought once from the App Store. Apple handles the transaction; the developer receives no personal information about you.

Children

There is no account, no chat, no user-generated content, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app is suitable for all ages, and it is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the app's behaviour changes with it, and the new version is published on this page. A policy that describes something the app does not do would be a defect in the app.
